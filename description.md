# Agent Usage

Requires WNotch 0.8.1 or newer.

See your Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Grok CLI, and Cursor accounts in one WNotch tab. Each account shows its plan, reported usage, reset time, and when it was last refreshed. Open Details for secondary limits and Codex banked-reset expiries.

Sign in through the providers' own clients, detect existing local accounts, and keep separate Codex or Claude accounts where supported. Privacy mode hides email addresses until you click to reveal them.

No passwords are collected. Missing provider data stays unavailable rather than becoming an estimated limit.
